exhibition: Fil-O-PiNOS

Overview

Fil‑O‑PiNOs, a devised showcase at NYU Tisch, explored Filipino-American identity through personal narratives, history, and physical metaphors. The show fused storytelling, movement, and cultural reflection in intimate theatrical form.

Jennifer joined the ensemble as a Filipina daughter, interweaving her own experiences into the piece. With vulnerability and cultural nuance, she voiced generational memory — from inherited traditions to the ache of belonging.

Her performance earned attention from the Kennedy Center, highlighting how she transforms collective experience into heartfelt theatre. Jennifer’s contribution was personal, precise, and emotionally generous.
